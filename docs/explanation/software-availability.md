# Software availability and reproducibility

The H&H Ensemble Modeling Toolkit (hhemt) is publicly available at <https://github.com/lassiterdc/hhemt> and on PyPI (`pip install hhemt`).

**License.** The source is licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or any later version](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html) (AGPL-3.0-or-later; see `LICENSE`). Release 0.1.0, on PyPI and Zenodo, was published under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

**How to cite.** See [How to cite](../index.md#how-to-cite) on the Home page.

**Reproducibility.** [Installation](../how-to/installation.md) reproduces the environment, and the two test tiers [Testing the toolkit](testing-the-toolkit.md#two-tiers-and-why-the-split-does-not-depend-on-your-machine) describes guard regressions.

**Data availability.** The Norfolk case-study inputs are published on HydroShare and fetched through the toolkit's integrated `case.yaml` path; [the Quickstart](../tutorials/quickstart.md#2-get-the-norfolk-example-data) says what the download needs from you.

**Reproduce the release.** Follow the [Norfolk end-to-end tutorial](../tutorials/norfolk-end-to-end.md).
