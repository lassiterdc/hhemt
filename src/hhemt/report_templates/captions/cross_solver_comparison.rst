**Purpose:** this figure compares SOLVER VERSIONS rather than compute configurations — it answers *did the solver change move the measurement?* There is one grouped panel per model arm (TRITON, TRITON-SWMM). Within a panel the x axis is the compute configuration and each bar series is one solver, identified by the pinned solver ref its bundles carry. The legend names a solver ``{sha8}_{YYYYMMDD}``, the first eight characters of its pin and the committer date of the commit that was compiled, when that date was captured at build time; a solver whose date was not captured is named by its pin prefix alone. Read the x axis across as configurations and the legend down as solver versions, oldest first: the series order is chronological by that captured commit date, NOT by the sha prefix, so a newer solver can carry a smaller sha and still sit later in the legend. An ``x`` marker above the bars at a given config means ``not_run`` — that solver has no simulation at that config, which is not a measured zero — and the config is kept on the x axis so the absence stays visible instead of disappearing from the figure. A series whose legend name carries no date is still a solver version: its commit date was not captured, so it is listed after every dated series, ordered by its pin, and its position says nothing about whether it is older or newer than the dated ones. Bar height is the read-model's single metric in the read-model's own unit, which the y-axis title names; the figure projects neither a derived ratio nor a normalised score. Projected from the ``cross_solver_comparison.json`` read-model written at combine time beside the merged tree, so re-rendering from a shipped bundle needs no re-merge.
